Frequently Asked

Questions brokers ask.

Everything about Property Media for Bellingham listings — pricing, process, platforms, and what's actually changing in real estate marketing.

What is Property Media and why does it matter for my listings?

Property Media is the digital media layer that sits on top of your photography — Matterport 3D tour for MLS and Realtor.com, listing video for Zillow and social, floor plan for the buyers who want to see the layout. It matters because buyer behavior has shifted: 62% of buyers actively seek listings with 3D tours (Zillow Consumer Housing Trends Report), and Bellingham's heavy out-of-area buyer market — Vancouver BC, Seattle, California, Arizona — makes rich digital media even more important. Most of these buyers form opinions on listings before they ever fly up.

Do I still need a photographer?

Yes. Property Media works alongside your photographer, not as a replacement. Photos are required on every listing — they handle the static stills that go in MLS, listing cards, and printed materials. Property Media handles everything after that: the 3D walkthrough, the video, the floor plan. You keep using your photographer; we add the digital experience on top.

How does this compare to hiring separate vendors for each piece?

For most brokers, the real choice is photographer-only versus photographer plus Property Media. A handful of photographers offer Matterport as an add-on and some offer video, but few specialize in real estate marketing across every platform with 48-hour delivery on all pieces. The Property Media bundle gives you a 3D tour, listing video, and floor plan from one shoot, delivered together, built for the way buyers actually search now.

Will my Matterport tour show on Zillow?

Not currently. Zillow and Matterport's parent company (CoStar) are in a dispute, and Matterport tours have been removed from Zillow since October 2025. Your tour still works on MLS, Realtor.com, Homes.com, and your own website — every major platform except Zillow. For Zillow specifically, the listing video and floor plan cover the gap (both display natively on Zillow). This is why most brokers go with the bundle instead of standalone tour.

How does AI search affect my listings?

Real estate platforms are integrating AI search faster than the broader industry. Zillow's ChatGPT app, Realtor.com's ChatGPT search, and Redfin's AI tools all launched between October 2025 and March 2026 — and they surface listings with rich structured data (3D tours, floor plans, video metadata) more often than listings with photos alone. Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT also pull from these listing sources. The shift is early but accelerating — listings with full Property Media are positioned for the AI-search era. Listings with photos only become harder to find as AI search adoption grows.

What size listings is this best for?

Property Media works on any listing size, but the value proposition is strongest for: listings priced $500K and above; listings that take more than two weeks to pend; listings expected to draw out-of-area buyers (Vancouver BC, Seattle, California, Arizona retirees); and larger homes where buyers want to understand layout before visiting. For fast-pending hot listings or sub-$400K starter homes, standalone Matterport may be enough.

Will Property Media help me win more listings?

Yes. 74% of agents using Matterport report winning more listings versus those who don't (Matterport client research). The pitch to sellers shifts from "I'll list your home" to "I market your home with full digital coverage on every major platform." Sellers see a tangible difference between a basic listing and a Property Media listing — it's a measurable competitive edge in your listing presentations.

How quickly will I see results?

The visible result is buyer engagement — listings with virtual tours get 87% more views and 5x more time on listing pages than photo-only listings. Translating that into faster pendings or higher prices depends on the listing, the market, and the buyer pool. National data shows tours close listings 31% faster on average, but Bellingham's specifics vary.

Do you work outside Bellingham?

Yes — Bellingham and broader Whatcom County are the primary service area. I've shot listings in Lynden and across Whatcom County. For listings outside Whatcom (Skagit, San Juan, etc.), call or text and we'll figure it out — travel pricing applies for farther shoots.

Can I see examples before committing?

Yes — the Gallery page shows recent listings with live interactive tours. The Scarlett Way listing is the strongest example of the Pro 250 quality. Active and recently sold listings span Bellingham and surrounding Whatcom County. For listing video samples, the portfolio is currently being built — ask about being featured.

What if I just want one piece, not the bundle?

Every service stands alone. Matterport tour starts at $200, listing video is $250 flat, floor plan is $49. The bundle saves $99 because most brokers end up wanting multiple pieces — but if you only need the 3D tour, that's fine.

When should I book my shoot?

Best practice is to book the shoot once staging is complete, photography is done, and the property is clean. If your timeline is tight, I can shoot the same day as photography — just call to coordinate. For listings in renovation or major repair, we usually wait until the work is finished so the tour represents what buyers will see.
