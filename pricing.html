FAQ

How fast can you shoot?

Most listings shot within 48 hours of booking. Same-day shoots often possible if I'm in your area — call to check.

Standard delivery is the next business day, with a 48-hour guarantee. Most tours are live within 24 hours of the shoot.

No. As a licensed Washington real estate agent with my own Supra eKey, I can access lockbox listings independently. You'll get a confirmation when I arrive and when I leave.

What if my listing is over 5,000 sq ft?

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Free reschedule anytime — real estate moves fast. Just let me know as soon as you can and we'll find a new time.

Do you do photography too?

No — Matterport tours only. I focus on doing one thing exceptionally well rather than spreading thin across services.
